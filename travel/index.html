Visitor Journey Simulation

FORM IRI-T4 · PRE-ARRIVAL RISK REHEARSAL · REV. 11

Prospective visitors to the Portuguese Republic are required to complete a simulated journey before arrival. The simulation models your traveller, your route and your provisions, and returns an assessment. Results are binding.

Notice: The Institute does not simulate return journeys.
